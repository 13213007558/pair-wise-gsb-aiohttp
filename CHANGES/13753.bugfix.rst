Hardened the client tracing life cycle: every request now emits exactly
one terminal signal (``on_request_end`` or ``on_request_exception``, never
both), ``on_dns_resolvehost_end``, ``on_connection_create_end`` and
``on_connection_queued_end`` are always paired with their start
counterparts even on failure, and exceptions raised by trace callbacks
are logged to the ``aiohttp.client`` logger and swallowed instead of
changing the outcome of the request -- by :user:`gsb-agent`.
